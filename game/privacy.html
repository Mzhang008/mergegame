Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The short version

Sakura Merge has no accounts, ads, purchases or cookies. Your progress stays on your device. The game may send anonymous usage counts that can't identify you, and you can switch that off.

Your saved game

Your progress (items, coins, village, settings) is saved in your browser's local storage on this device. It is never sent to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser, or choosing Menu › Settings › Reset progress, deletes it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the game people enjoy, the game can count anonymous events, such as starting a session, finishing the tutorial, restoring a part of the village, or coming back a day or a week after first playing. These counts are sent to GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service that uses no cookies and does not track you across websites.

  • Each count includes the event name and your screen width. Like any web request, it also carries your browser's user-agent string and your IP address, which GoatCounter uses for basic statistics such as browser type and country. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.
  • We never send your name, contact details, saved game, or anything you type.
  • Nothing is sent if you turn off Menu › Settings › Share anonymous usage stats, or if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.
  • If that switch isn't shown in Settings, statistics aren't enabled in your version of the game and nothing is sent.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Hosting

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information about requests to its servers, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Mzhang008/mergegame/issues.
